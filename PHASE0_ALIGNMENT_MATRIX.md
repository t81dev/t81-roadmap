Phase 0 can close only when:

1. Terminology disagreements are resolved with a single canonical glossary path.
2. Formal-vs-interpretive source-of-truth boundaries are published and linked from `README.md` in affected repos.
3. Constraint register exists with explicit owners and review cadence.
4. Benchmark publication cadence includes at least one dated publication-cycle issue with linked artifacts.
